# Third-party notices

`third_party/fast_dllm_v1/` is the pinned Fast-dLLM v1 source used for the
compatibility adapter. Its upstream README, paper link, commit and revision
metadata are retained in that directory. The Apache-2.0 license text is
included at `third_party/LICENSE-APACHE-2.0`; distribution remains subject to
any additional upstream notices and terms.

`third_party/llada_generate.py` contains the small upstream LLaDA generation
utility needed by the full model adapter. The SETCOMMIT selector itself is in
`setcommit/` and is covered by the top-level MIT license. The retained
`dacs_repro/` directory is a compatibility copy for older internal runners.
